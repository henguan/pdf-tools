Help Center

1. Client-Side PDF Tool User Guide (Local Processing Only)

1.1 PPT to PDF (Local Processing)

  1. Go to the homepage and click the "PPT to PDF" tab;
  2. Click "Choose PPT File" to select a local .ppt or .pptx file (drag & drop supported);
  3. The file will be converted locally in your browser (no server upload);
  4. Click "Download File" to save the converted PDF.

1.2 PDF Compression (Local Processing)

  1. Click the "PDF Compress" tab on the homepage;
  2. Select a local PDF file (drag & drop supported);
  3. Compression completes locally in seconds;
  4. Click "Download File" to get the compressed PDF.

1.3 Image to PDF (JPG / PNG)

  1. Select "JPG to PDF" or "PNG to PDF" on the homepage;
  2. Click "Choose Images" to upload single or multiple image files;
  3. All conversions run locally in your browser;
  4. Click the download button to export the PDF file.

1.4 PDF Merge & Split

  1. Merge PDF: Select multiple PDF files, they will be combined into one file;
  2. Split PDF: Upload a PDF and enter page ranges (e.g. 1,3,5-8) to split;
  3. All operations are 100% local, no files are uploaded to any server.

1.5 Is My Data Safe?

  1. All files are processed locally in your browser;
  2. No files are uploaded to any server;
  3. All temporary data is deleted automatically when you close the tab.
